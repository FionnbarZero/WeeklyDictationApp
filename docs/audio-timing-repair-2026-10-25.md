## What changed

The recorded-word player attaches its media listeners only after the downloaded word replaces the silent gesture primer and load() cancels the previous media resource. Native recording completion additionally requires playback to have started. A primer ending during a pending fetch can no longer report the real word as completed. Gesture activation, start/completion timeouts, Report interruption/resume, download cancellation and temporary URL cleanup are retained.

Shuriken's card-audio outcome now carries its request identity and success status. Pending turn feedback requires successful playback, the still-current request and a mounted activity. Exit and completion invalidate the request synchronously, before cancellation promises can settle. A failed, superseded or exited word cannot play old feedback. Report/return can still finish the interrupted word and play normal feedback. Teaching, timers, scoring, saves and curriculum policies are unchanged. The changed behavior remains in the isolated-preview path; the default production build stays within its unchanged size gates.
